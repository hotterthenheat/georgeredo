## Conclusion

Phase 3 closes the two blockers that waited on a token and opens none: the live chain's IVs carry 4 %, so the one thing in the terminal that could have been wrong by a vol point was right, and the one real finding of the static checks was a duplicated `/ 1000`, now impossible by construction. The two hypotheses sharpen Phase 2's lesson rather than add to it. E shows what a significance rule does that a sign rule does not: a lift that is +0.7 % inside a ±1.6-point interval in the period it was fitted on and +9 % with a clear interval in the single period read afterwards is, by the rule written before the read, a post-hoc observation, and its stress reads name the mechanism — the model's quietest minutes are the mornings before an event it cannot see, and whether those afternoons were the condor's worst or best days decided the sign of the whole period. F shows the limit of pre-registration: fixing the rule and the fill model before this code touched 2025–2026 cannot undo that the cell was named on that same data, so a four-part pass on n 879 is a conditionally justified rule to confirm forward, and the confirmation's cost is set by the session SD, not by wishes — 451 sessions at the NBBO mean, 1,276 under a cent of slippage. The one quantity both hypotheses agree on is the unconditional afternoon SPY 0DTE variance premium, paid for in friction of 41–57 % of the gross, and the one measurement neither can make is the realized complex-order fill, which is what the tracker exists to log.
