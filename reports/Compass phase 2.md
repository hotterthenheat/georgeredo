# Phase 2 Finds Premium, Not Signals

Four pre-registered hypotheses on 0DTE defined-risk short premium and volatility — every leg at its own NBBO, $0.65 a leg each way, picked on 2022–2024 and read once on 2025–2026 — return **three "no edge" verdicts and one "needs X"**, and the two cells that read positive out of sample are unconditional short premium on SPY, not the signals the hypotheses were about. The low-moc_mag closing pin (A) flipped from +$26.3 a spread on fit (n 252) to **−$33.0 [−83.8, +12.6] out of sample (n 177, PF 0.63, both years negative)**, with none of 1,458 SPXW cells clear of zero; the straddle-boundary touch (B) adds nothing over the same sale at the same time of day (its increment −0.97 % of max risk in fit, +1.47 % out of sample — a sign flip); the debit vertical (C) keeps a smaller loss than the naked contract (+9 to +16 points of debit) but never a premium, since the audit's entries have no direction; the volatility model's top decile (D) realises the trade-relevant target at 0.537 against 0.539 for a baseline that knows only the clock and the straddle, and every long-premium cell is negative or inside the noise. Two candidates survive as results, not rules: B's **midday SPY 2-wide iron condor** (pre-registered, +3.25 % of max risk OOS with a lower bound of +$0.03, its unfiltered parent +3.13 % [+$0.61, +$9.61], friction 40–45 % of the gross) and C's **iron ±5 butterfly on SPY/QQQ at 15:00 and 15:30** (post hoc — found after the OOS read, +5.0 % and +6.0 % of risk with CIs clear, every year positive, alive at $0.02 a leg worse fills). Nothing from Phase 2 goes into the engine; what the data points toward is the 0DTE variance premium on SPY, paid for in friction, confirmable only forward.

## The recorder: seven routes, 24 names, about 1,500 calls a day, eight things unsettled

`scripts/eod_recorder.py` is the Module 11 and strike-book recorder the audit and the spec call for: one Python process (3.11+, polars, nothing else), append-only, with an intraday scan loop and a post-close pass, built on 2026-10-21 from the UW docs on disk, the terminal's typed routes and the lake's conventions, **with no vendor call made while building it** ([eod_recorder.README.md](../scripts/eod_recorder.README.md)). The scan loop asks `GET /api/stock/{t}/spot-exposures/strike` — the whole book by strike, every expiry together, as UW figured it — at 09:35, every half hour from 10:00 to 15:30, and 15:50 (14 scans; ~491 rows a scan on SPY), which is the per-minute-king and wall history the audit could not test. The 16:15 pass records, per name: every flow alert of $25,000 and up off the market route `/api/option-trades/flow-alerts` with `ticker_symbol` (the per-name route answers without `id`, `start_time` or `end_time`), every field a live answer carried — `has_sweep`, `has_floor`, `total_ask_side_prem`, `total_bid_side_prem`, `volume_oi_ratio`, the greeks — so the $100k whale threshold itself can be tested; every dark-pool print off `/api/darkpool/{t}` with its `nbbo_bid`, `nbbo_ask`, `size`, `premium`, `market_center` and sale conditions, the print ≥ ask / ≤ bid signature left to the reader; the day's `net-prem-ticks` (~390 rows); the day's `ohlc/1m` (~750 bars, pre-market to after hours); open interest by strike off `oi-per-strike?date=D` (`greek-exposure/strike` carries no OI field at all, so it was not used); and the chain off `option-contracts?exclude_zero_oi_chains=true` (~9,900 SPY contracts in 20 pages, with `open_interest`, `prev_oi`, IV, the day's volume by side and `last_tape_time`). The count dated D is the positions at the close of the session before D, published about 06:45; the README says so and says which row a reader wants. The universe is SPX, SPY, QQQ, IWM and the audit's top 20 roots by option volume (NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, MU, INTC, AMZN, TLT, META, EWZ, PLTR, AMD, IBIT, MSFT, GOOGL, MSTR, HYG, AVGO, SOFI, ORCL, GME) — 24 names; an index is asked as `SPX` for its book, not asked for prints or bars (422 on Basic), and its flow alerts asked under both `SPX` and `SPXW`. Every column lands as the vendor's string exactly as sent, one Parquet file a name a New York day, six stamps first (`_day`, `_scan`, `_scan_at`, `_fetched_at`, `_name`, `_key`); rows are de-duplicated on the vendor's own id with the first row kept and nothing ever deleted; a marker per (endpoint, name, day, scan) makes a re-run free; a `partial` marker names its reason and is fetched again.

The budget is the brief's and is enforced: **at most 3 calls in flight (UW Basic's measured ceiling), a token bucket of 100 calls a minute, and a daily budget of 20,000 calls counted per New York day in a file that survives a restart**; 429 waits the `Retry-After` or 2-4-8-16 s, 5xx the same, five tries then partial; any other 4xx is recorded and passed over, a 422 on `limit` retried once at the fallback (darkpool 500 → 200, alerts 200 → 100). The arithmetic for 24 names: scans 336, flow alerts ~75, dark pool 300–900, net-prem-ticks 24, bars 23, OI 24, chain 250–480 — **about 1,100–1,900 calls a day, under a tenth of the budget and under 1.2 % of the plan's 160,000; with every cap hit 11,641, still under 20,000.** Seventeen tests on a fake client and a fake clock pass: two runs add no duplicate and the second makes no call; the limiter paces and holds three in flight; the budget stops with its STOP line; the token never appears in a log, marker, failure record or error; every column is a string; the schedule handles weekends, Thanksgiving, the half day and `--auto`'s window. A full `--eod --dry-run` for 24 names is 144 jobs, 378 calls, 108,658 rows; the repeat skips all 144. The token comes from `UW_API_TOKEN` in the environment only — the script refuses to start a live mode without it and never reads a repository file for it; the Windows restart loop (`eod_recorder.cmd`, for Task Scheduler) loads it from a file outside the repository, the systemd unit from an `EnvironmentFile` at mode 600, cron sources it.

**Unsettled until the smoke test** — each coded on the best reading and marked in the script's `Endpoint.note`: (1) `flow-alerts` paging — `limit=200` assumed with 100 as the fallback, `older_than` assumed to compare `created_at`, and an echoed `newer_than` two months back suggests a 60-day window, which bounds any backfill; (2) `darkpool/{t}` paging — `limit=500` asked first, whether `date=D` alone bounds the day, and SPY's per-day print count (the cap is 250 pages, 50,000 prints); (3) `spot-exposures/strike` paging — the terminal read 491 rows in one answer with no `page`; if the route ignores `page` the cap of 10 ends it with a `partial` marker and the fix is `paging='none'`; (4) an index — whether `oi-per-strike`, `option-contracts` and `net-prem-ticks` answer for SPX, whether the chain holds SPXW's contracts, and which root carries the flow alerts; (5) `ohlc/1m` at `limit=1000` (746 bars seen live); (6) the chain's page count (20 on SPY, cap 40); (7) the rate ceilings — three in flight measured, the rest the brief's; (8) holidays past 2027 and any early close beyond the two listed. The first run is the owner's: `python3 -I scripts/eod_recorder.py --smoke --name SPY` (one call a route, about seven calls, written under `<root>/smoke/`, never the real store), then `--smoke --name SPX`, each read against the eight points; then the first real `--eod` after 16:15 New York and `--status`; then the daemon.

## Conclusion

Phase 2 narrows the audit's conclusion rather than overturning it. The audit found that every long-premium rule returns the premium's decay plus the spread; Phase 2 finds that the mirror is not a free edge either — a signal that predicts the close's volatility (moc_mag, ρ +0.18 to +0.25), the band's touch, or a volatility model's top decile is priced into the credit or the debit before the trade is placed, and what is left after the NBBO and $0.65 a leg is the unconditional 0DTE variance premium on SPY and nothing on SPXW at 5–25 points wide. Three of the four pre-registrations held their own fate on record (A's pick flipped sign, D's best-on-fit and opening-drive cells flipped sign, C's verticals were expected negative and were), and the one lesson about method is that a rule asking for a positive lift rather than a significant one let A's +1.34 % [−0.03, +2.87] through, and that C's pre-registration was made from a pooled table that hid the per-name iron ±5 — which is why that cell, the strongest number in Phase 2 (+6.0 % [+3.4, +8.4] of risk at 15:30 OOS, every year positive, alive at two cents a leg), is a candidate and not a rule. The engine takes nothing from Phase 2: no fly, no condor, no cut by moc_mag, no touch, no decile.

What the owner must do next is four things, in order. **First, the GET**: with a working token, run the audit's `q1c` script against `stock/SPY/option-contracts` at an expiry about 30 days out with `stock-state`, and only then patch trailField — if the live chain is r = 0, the terminal's puts are 1–2 vol points too rich today. **Second, the smoke test**: `--smoke --name SPY` then `--name SPX`, settle the eight points, run the first `--eod`, register the daemon; the Module 11 gates, the walls, the true king and B4 become testable after a recorded year, picked on the first and read on the second. **Third, the forward paper record**, the only confirmation this data cannot give: the SPY midday 2-wide em1 condor on at least 60 sessions with real complex-order fills plus the $0.25 settlement check and a 120-session re-read with the rule unchanged, and the SPY/QQQ iron ±5 fly at 15:30 (15:00 second) on at least 250 sessions a name after 2026-10-09 under its fixed pass criteria — fail on any one. **Fourth**, if the owner wants the next hypothesis, it is the one both A and D point at and neither tested: unconditional short 0DTE premium on SPY held to the close, and the short-premium reading of the volatility model at its bottom decile — each needing its own pre-registration with a significance requirement on the lift, not a sign.
