Compass: final results and parameters

Research summary as of 2026-10-22. Every number is from a committed, pre-registered study in the georgeredo repository (branch claude/lucid-heisenberg-bpyq7k); commit hashes are given so each can be checked.

Bottom line.
  • No strategy tested is ready to trade with real money. None has passed its pre-registered test and its forward confirmation.
  • Buying calls and puts loses after costs: about −13% of the debit per trade on the 0DTE, index and ETF base trades, and about −23% on 31–60 DTE single-stock options.
  • No direction signal tested beats that cost: UW flow and sweeps, net premium, NOPE, market tide, dark pool, GEX regime, walls and flip, opening range, prior-day levels, and post-earnings drift.
  • Three rules reduce the loss and passed a held-out test: a spread veto, a SPXW-vs-SPY venue choice and a 14:00 exit on 0DTE. Together they cut the loss from about −11% to about −4% per trade. They don't make long options profitable.
  • The short-premium paper candidates (iron fly, 20-delta condor) looked positive at the official close, but fail once after-hours assignment risk is priced.

Δ = −0.90 points [−3.53, +1.71]: Fail. Mean per trade: −24.3% on the picked side, −22.5% on the other. The holdout (1,600 names never opened by any study) was not fetched and stays clean for one future test. The overnight-reversal hypothesis does not exist as a study, so no options version was added.

Rule (b101ae1): each model is judged once, after exactly N complete forward sessions from 2026-10-22. It is confirmed if Δ > 0 with the 20-session block interval clear of zero. N gives 80% power for an effect the size the holdout showed. The forward set is SPY, QQQ and SPXW 0DTE, so about 1–2.3 years at 252 sessions a year.

3.2 Base trades the models are judged against (PREREG_FEATURES.md)

  • 0DTE: entries at 09:45, 10:15 … 15:15, 15:30. Exit at the 15:58 bid; SPXW settles on the official SPX close.
  • 1–7 DTE: held to expiry. 8–30 DTE: closing bid 5 sessions later. 31–60 DTE: closing bid 10 sessions later. Entry at the closing ask; the expiry nearest 4, 19 or 45 days.
  • Strike buckets, in implied-move units (ATM straddle mid ÷ spot): ITM −0.5 to −0.15, ATM ±0.15, OTM1 0.15–0.5, OTM2 0.5–1.0, OTM3 1.0–1.5.
  • Liquidity tiers, measured on the 20 sessions before each month: Tier 1 is volume ≥ 900k and spread ≤ 5%; Tier 3 is volume < 400k or spread > 10%.

4. What is running

  • The daily forward job runs in this container every weekday at 7:47 pm New York. It downloads the day's data, checks and merges it, replays the paper tracker, and scores the six frozen models once Cboe publishes the official SPX close.
  • Each run commits the updated cumulative report and the forward files. There is no verdict before each model's N.

5. Caveats

  • The UW key is a trial key: trade-by-trade flow history reaches back only 30 days, so flow was tested through UW's flow alerts. UW serves no past intraday GEX book, so walls and the flip are the previous session's.
  • The feature study's improvement-round passes are weaker evidence, because the holdout had already been read for their components.
  • All results are research. None is investment advice or a recommendation to trade.
