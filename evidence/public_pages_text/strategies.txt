 Position sizing and strikes
 Every strategy sizes positions and picks strikes to fit the account. 
 Position sizing 
 Contract counts scale with the account balance: 
 Small (<$5k): 1-3 contracts (base sizing)
 Medium ($5k–$10k): Scaled proportionally
 Large (≥$10k): Risk-based sizing (7-15 contracts, capped at 50)
 Risk per trade
 0DTE: 0.8%–1.5% 
 Swing: 2.0%–3.0% 
 Strike selection 
 Strikes move closer to the money as the account grows: 
 Large: ITM/ATM (delta 0.40–0.70) for better R:R
 Medium: ATM (delta 0.30–0.55)
 Small: OTM (delta 0.20–0.40) for leverage
 By time of day
 Morning: Lower delta (0.20-0.30) 
 Afternoon: Medium delta (0.40-0.50) 
 Power Hour: Higher delta (0.50-0.60)
