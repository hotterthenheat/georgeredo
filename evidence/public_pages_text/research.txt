 The Morning Squeeze whale-hold edge was a lookahead artifact
 A ten-month census looked like a real edge — whale-held contracts returned +23.5% against −9.0% without the whale. Re-running the same flat files and removing the lookahead, one defect at a time, collapses the +32.5-point whale gap to −0.4 points, and the strategy loses money in every block.
