 Buy & Forget and Full size
 Buy & Forget means the print is worth following and holding — George tells you to get out when the buyer sells, when the contract is down 75%, or five minutes before the bell. Full size is George's highest-conviction call — max conviction, not a whole-account instruction. Hold tracks every one of them and says when to get out.

 A late-day flush into the close that snaps back. George waits for the bounce to hold on closed bars, then names the exact call or put, its live price, and a stop at the option's wick low. It is 0DTE into the bell — spreads and time decay are brutal.
