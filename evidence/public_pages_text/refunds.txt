 Policy Overview 
 All paid subscriptions include a 24-hour free trial . After the trial period, all sales are final with no refunds. 
 01 24-Hour Free Trial
 All paid plans include a 24-hour free trial. Your payment method is collected at checkout but will not be charged until the trial period ends. If you cancel within the first 24 hours, you will not be charged.
 02 No Refunds After Trial
 Once the 24-hour trial period has ended and your payment has been processed, all sales are final. There are no refunds, partial or otherwise, for any subscription period.
 03 Cancellation
 You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings or the Stripe customer portal. Cancellation will take effect at the end of your current billing period, and you will continue to have access until then.
 04 Billing Issues
 If you experience a billing issue (e.g., duplicate charge, incorrect amount), please open a support ticket in our Discord server. We will investigate and resolve legitimate billing errors promptly.
 05 Price Changes
 We may change subscription prices. Any change applies to new subscriptions and to renewals beginning at least 30 days after we notify you by email.
 06 Existing Subscribers
 While your subscription remains continuously active, the price you signed up at will not increase, including when you upgrade, downgrade, or switch billing interval. This rate is tied to your subscription: if it is cancelled or lapses, resubscribing is at then-current pricing.
