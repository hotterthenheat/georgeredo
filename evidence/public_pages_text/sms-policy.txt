 About the program
 George FI Corporation Alerts: account notifications for existing subscribers — we text you when the software detects activity on a tracked contract, or publishes an entry setup, BUY & FORGET signal or GET OUT alert.
 Message frequency varies with market activity, up to 10 messages per day.
 Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
 Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel — or manage alerts any time from your account page.
 Notifications describe market activity and software signals, not trades in your account. They are not financial advice, and they never contain offers, promotions or performance claims.
 How you opt in
 Enrollment happens only on your logged-in account page , in three steps: you enter your mobile number, you actively check the consent box below (it is never pre-checked), and you confirm a one-time 6-digit verification code we text to that number. No number receives alerts without completing all three steps. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any George FI Corporation service.
