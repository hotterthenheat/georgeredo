# External research register — retail-accessible option edges (2026-10-15)

Source: an eight-lens literature, practitioner and data sweep (`research_notes/Retail options edge sweep/`, 1.1 MB of cited notes; 42 hypotheses in `_captured_hypotheses_and_verdicts.json`). The adversarial verification stage completed 15 of 126 skeptic checks (L1–L5 of the academic lens, three each: all refuted) before the model usage limit; the rest was stopped to conserve the session budget, so every hypothesis below other than those five is **unverified** and enters the registry only as a pre-registration candidate, never as a result.

## 1. What the sweep establishes

- **Directional option signals do not survive retail costs.** Option momentum, skew/IV-spread stock predictors, characteristic-sorted delta-hedged returns, order-imbalance reversal and lottery-option avoidance are real in the gross literature but are zero or negative at 100 % of the quoted half-spread in the papers' own after-cost tables (skeptic verdicts on L1–L5). This agrees with every directional test in this registry (S01–S16, S06-TierA).
- **Dealer-positioning effects are volatility effects, not direction.** Public-OI gamma predicts absolute moves (H5), not signed ones; pinning (P4) and expiry-day gamma (P2) are smaller than an option round trip.
- **The positive retail expectation that is documented is premium harvesting:** index put-writing (Cboe PUT/WPUT/BXMD methodologies, 2012–2026 records), selling earnings-event straddles in the most liquid names (Dubinsky-Johannes-Kaeck-Seeger; de Silva-Smith-So), and the event-eve variance premium on scheduled macro days. All are equity-like or short-tail risk with high win rates and small average trades; none is alpha in the sense of a directional edge.
- **Execution is the largest single lever available:** resting midpoint (or one-tick-inside) limit orders halve the spread side of the round trip with 45–70 % fill probability (X1, L1), and documented retail price improvement makes the registry's ask-in/bid-out fills the worst case (R1). That shifts every option result in this registry up by roughly $0.5–1 per ETF contract and $6–30 per SPXW structure; it changes no sign found so far.
- **Base rate:** retail option traders lose on average, mostly through spreads, overpaying for event volatility and holding losers (retail-performance notes). A retail strategy has to clear that base rate before it has an edge.

## 4. Closed or refuted (do not re-propose)

- Refuted by three skeptics each: L1 VIX-slope straddle timing; L2 cross-sectional option momentum; L3 characteristic-sorted delta-hedged returns; L4 lottery-option avoidance as an edge; L5 option-implied stock predictors.
- Below an option round trip by the lens arithmetic: P1–P6 (dealer gamma, pinning, last-30-minute momentum, flow sign), L6 weekend decay (a timing filter only), L7 order-imbalance reversal, E4 pre-earnings IV-spread direction, the OPEN rows for S12 (QQQ first half hour) and S16 (prior-day fade).
