## Verdict and notes (written from the tables above; nothing was tuned)

**T-C FAILS the gate as pre-registered (C1, C3, C4 fail; C2 passes).** The 13:00 SPXW ATM short straddle, sold at the NBBO bid and bought back at the NBBO ask at 15:55 with $1.00 per contract per side, made $24,554 over 339 sessions, a mean of **+$72.4 per straddle-day with a date-bootstrap 95 % interval of [−75.8, +214.2]** (win rate 63.4 %, profit factor 1.16, maximum drawdown −$21,180, worst day 2026-06-11 at −$8,499). Excluding the 40 FOMC/CPI/NFP days lowers it to +$45.3 [−103.1, +182.5] (n = 299). The 15:59 exit (+$81.7 [−71.3, +231.3]) and the 16:00 last-quote exit (+$36.1 [−125.1, +192.0]) do not change the picture; the 2.0× credit stop triggered on 55 sessions and reduced the mean to +$53.2 [−79.3, +185.9] while cutting the drawdown to −$15,020.

**Spreads are not the problem.** The realized half-spread at the 13:00 entry is 0.87 % of the straddle mid on average (median 0.81 %, 90th percentile 1.31 %, maximum 2.25 %), i.e. 0.05–0.10 points per leg on a credit that averages 18.3 points: well inside the 1.5 % gate, and far tighter than the 1.5 % assumed for SPX-class contracts in the plan. The exit is where the spread bites: at 15:55 the straddle half-spread averages 0.50 points (3.8 % of the remaining mid), because the losing leg is quoted 0.00/0.05 and the winning leg has a 0.4–1.3 point half-spread; the mean round-trip cost is $68.9 per straddle (entry + exit half-spreads × 100 + $4 fees). The mid-to-mid reference, +$141.3 [−5.1, +281.7], is $69 above the executable number, which is exactly that cost, so the executable result is the mid result minus the measured spread, as C4 requires one to check.

**Random-entry baseline.** Five committed draws of one random entry minute per session (09:35–14:30, seed 11) give means of +$59.0, −$119.8, +$105.1, +$77.8 and +$37.0 (pooled +$31.8 [−118.8, +176.8], n = 1,695; the −$119.8 draw holds entries on 2025-11-20 and 2025-10-10 that lost $18,489 and $17,349). The 13:00 lane beats the per-date mean of the five draws by +$40.6 [−73.4, +164.2], less than one mean round-trip cost ($68.9) and with an interval covering zero: C3 fails.

**Hold-to-settlement variant (not the pre-registered design).** Holding to cash settlement at the official SPX close removes the exit spread and one fee: +$156.8 per day with interval [+5.8, +305.0] on all sessions (win rate 67.3 %, PF 1.35, max drawdown −$23,720), but [−18.5, +288.6] ex-events (n = 299). The lower bound is marginal, depends on keeping the event days, and the variant carries full overnight-free but intraday-unlimited risk into the close (the June 2026 cluster: 2026-06-05, 06-09, 06-11 and 06-17 lost $23,811 together). It is reported for completeness, not as a pass; under the plan's rules it would need its own pre-registration and out-of-sample window.
