# Methodology: GeorgeFi full-history reconstruction vs Unusual Whales 1-minute bars

## Data sources
- **GeorgeFi ledgers** (read-only): `normalized/trades.csv`, 1,149 rows (Epoch 1 main book 546, 2026-01-20 to 02-27; Epoch 2 main book 489, 2026-03-02 to 10-08; Epoch 2 side books trade_now_v1 55, singles_today_v1 48, spx_plan_v1 11), as normalised by `code/normalize.py` from the public `/api` pulls. `normalized/paper_trades_spx.csv`, 84 closed SPX paper trades (`spx_gex_engine`, 2026-07-15 to 10-07), mapped onto the same schema and treated as a separate group labelled `paper`. 17 paper rows carry `size = 0` (post-close size); the lot is implied as `pnl / ((exit − entry) × 100)` when that is a whole number (all 17 imply 1 contract); the 11 remaining paper P&L mismatches are partial exits (`T1 PARTIAL`) on 2-lot entries, flagged like the ledger's.
- **Unusual Whales REST** via `backtest/data.py` (`option-contract/{cid}/intraday?date=`, `stock/{ticker}/ohlc/1m?date=`), token from `UW_API_TOKEN` in the environment only, gzip cache in `$BT_CACHE`. Contract id = OCC without the `O:` prefix (e.g. `SPXW260227P06875000`). Bars carry open/high/low/close, `avg_price` (trade-weighted average of the minute, used as VWAP), side-tagged volumes and premiums. A bar exists only for minutes with at least one print. No Massive/Polygon calls were made.
- **Trading calendar**: `backtest/trading_days.json` (ends 2026-10-12), extended in memory to 2026-10-13 for the last ledger day.

## Fetch (code/fetch_bars.py)
For every option row the entry-date bars; for the 20 multi-day holds every trading day from entry through exit; for the 50 SHARES rows the underlying RTH 1-minute bars for entry and exit dates. 770 unique (contract, day) requests, 3 parallel workers (UW enforces a concurrency limit of 3), 588 network fetches + 181 cache hits, no request errors, 142 s. Per row: `bars_available` (entry day), `bars_available_exit_day`, `n_bars`, HTTP error code (UW returns `{'error': code}` on 400/404/422), whether a bar printed in the exact entry and exit minutes, entry-minute volume, and `missing_reason` ∈ {ok, ok (data_error row), no_occ, no bars (SPX root), http_422, empty_payload}. Plain-`SPX`-root ids return no bars; they are recorded, not substituted (a labelled SPXW-twin sensitivity is in `spx_root_substitution.csv`).

## Definitions
- Times: ledger `entryTime`/`exitTime` are UTC; converted to America/New_York. "Entry minute" = the bar whose start minute contains the entry timestamp.
- **A (Reported by GeorgeFi)**: `pnl_reported` as given. n, sum, win rate (P&L > 0), mean, bootstrap 95 % CI of the mean, max drawdown of cumulative P&L in exit-time order, profit factor, median hold minutes, median size. Also by month (ET entry date) and by OCC root. Repeated excluding data-error rows (`exit_reason` contains DATA ERROR/INVALID) and P&L-mismatch rows (|reported − (exit − entry) × 100 × size| > $1).
- **B (Simulated fills, ours)**: rows with a print in both the entry and the exit minute (strict; the next-bar fallback used in `evidence/scripts/validate_trades.py` is shown as a sensitivity). Entry = entry-minute bar `avg_price` (fallback close), exit = exit-minute bar `avg_price`; P&L = (exit − entry) × 100 × reported size (shares × 1). Cost models as `backtest/costs.py`: `mid` no cost; `spread` half-spread per side 1 % SPY/QQQ, 2 % IWM, 1.5 % SPXW/NDXP/RUTW (the `SPX` key), 2 % single names (runtime key `SINGLE`), plus $0.70 per contract per side; `adverse` buy at the entry bar high, sell at the exit bar low, plus fees. Shares: no spread, no fees (their total reported P&L is $3.76). Fill realism: reported entry (exit) premium inside the bar's [low, high]; position in the bar (premium − low)/(high − low); below/above the bar's low/high; entry below (exit above) the bar VWAP. "Reported − mid" is the per-trade difference between GeorgeFi's P&L and the bar-VWAP P&L on the same row.
- **C (Independent reconstruction, ours)**: 0DTE option rows (expiration = ET entry date) with entry-day bars. Entry = VWAP of the first bar strictly after the entry minute; the entry bar must start before 15:55 and be followed by at least one bar. Exits by `backtest/exits.py::simulate` with default parameters: `trail` (stop −25 %, breakeven lock after +6 % high-water mark, guardian trail 35 % give-back after +15 %, 30-minute time stop when not above entry, hard close at the first bar ≥ 15:55), `ladder` (trail + T1 +25 % half, T2 +45 %), `bracket` (±25 %), `hold` (15:55 close). Each leg settled with `backtest/costs.py::settle` for mid/spread/adverse (adverse there = buy entry-bar high, sell each leg at price × (1 − 2 × half-spread), + fees). Dollars are per contract (1 lot); "sized" multiplies by the reported size. Random baseline: for each actual trade, 20 uniformly random RTH minutes (09:30–15:53) on the same contract-day, same direction/contract (the contract fixes the direction), same engines and costs; "strategy − random" is the paired per-trade difference between the actual outcome and the mean of its 20 random draws, with a bootstrap CI. A time-matched variant draws the 20 minutes within ±30 minutes of the actual entry. MFE/MAE: max high / min low over the bars after the entry bar through 15:55, relative to the entry VWAP; the MFE percentile is the share of 50 random-minute entries on the same contract-day (uniform, and time-matched ±30 min) whose MFE is below the actual one (ties count half).
- **Clusters**: rows sharing contract and entry minute (scale-ins / repeated entries) are one decision. Every main table reports the number of such clusters and a cluster bootstrap CI (resample clusters, recompute the per-trade mean).
- **D**: all of the above split by epoch and by Epoch 2 book, plus hold, size, premium, exit-reason families (keyword classes: profit-take/trail, stop, time/eod, data_error, other), SWING/shares shares, cluster counts, and the four Epoch 1 survival questions.
- **E**: entry < exit, exits ≥ 16:00 ET, entries < 09:30 ET, 0DTE exits after 15:55, zero-volume entry bars, missing-print entry minutes, duplicate ids, same-contract-same-minute pairs, P&L mismatches and data errors; A/B totals with and without those rows.

## Statistics and seeds
Bootstrap: 1,000 resamples, `numpy.random.default_rng(7)` created afresh for every statistic (so each CI is independent of table order); percentile 2.5/97.5 of the resampled means. Random entry minutes: one `random.Random(11)` consumed in ledger order (ledger rows, then paper rows). Win rate = share of outcomes > 0; profit factor = gross wins / gross losses.

## Limitations
- No NBBO: bar VWAP is a mid proxy; the spread model is a flat assumption (index options at 1.5 % half-spread is a guess within the observed ranges, single names at 2 % are probably optimistic in the first and last 15 minutes).
- Bars exist only for minutes with prints: 138 rows have no print in the entry minute and are excluded from B (sensitivity with the next bar shown), which removes the least liquid fills, not the most.
- Multi-day swings (20 rows) are priced at the exit-day bar; C is restricted to 0DTE rows so SWING-account option rows (123 in Epoch 1, 79 in Epoch 2) and share rows are only in A/B.
- C fixes exits to mechanical rules the operator does not claim; it tests whether the reported entry minute carries information, not whether the operator's exits are reproducible. An entry minute chosen after the fact would pass this test; the bars cannot tell.
- Repeated same-minute entries inflate n; the cluster CIs are the ones to read for Epoch 1 (546 rows, 263 clusters).
- The paper book has no `initial_size`; partial exits are flagged but priced at the final size.
