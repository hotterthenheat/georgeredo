**What this does and does not show.** GeorgeFi's reported record (A) is the operator's own marking and is not evidence that its signals predict anything: it depends on fills that the tape frequently did not print (B) and, in Epoch 1, on repeated same-minute entries and exit-management rules that cannot be reproduced from the published data. B shows how much of the record is fill assumption and cost; it says nothing about signal quality because the exits are GeorgeFi's. Only C vs the random baseline speaks to entry timing, and it is a weak test: it assumes the entry minute was chosen in real time (an entry minute written after the fact would pass this test), it treats repeated same-minute entries as one decision only in the cluster CIs, it prices fills at bar VWAP without NBBO, and it fixes the exits to mechanical rules the operator does not claim to use. On that test, the Epoch 1 entries show a positive point estimate that is not robust to clustering and time-of-day matching, and the Epoch 2 and paper entries show none. Negative and null results here are results; nothing was tuned.
