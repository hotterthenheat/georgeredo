# Our evidence ledger — what this repository has already tested, what is closed, what is still open (internal lens, 2026-10-14)

Scope of this note: the repository's own evidence only. No web source is cited here because none was used; every number below is copied from `research/compass/registry.jsonl` (46 entries, last recorded 2026-10-14 19:20 UTC), `research/compass/05_results.md`, `03_reconciliation.md`, `09_final_specification.md`, `research/georgefi_full_history/results/G_edge_crossref.md`, `BACKTEST_RESULTS.md`, `COMPASS_RESEARCH_PLAN.md`, `02_coverage_matrix.md` and `07_coverage_report.md`, plus two artefacts that exist on disk but are not yet in the registry (the S06 Tier A screen table written 21:11 UTC and the S11 event-chain pull log). Evidence grade for everything in this note is "practitioner" (in-house studies, pre-registered under `04_protocol.md`, reproducible from committed scripts, not peer-reviewed). Every option fill in these studies is retail-realistic: marketable orders at the ThetaData 1-minute (or 1-second) NBBO — buy at the ask, sell at the bid — plus $0.70 per contract per side ($1.00 on SPXW in the H13/H15 grids); no rebates, no mid fills. Intervals are 95 % bootstrap intervals clustered by trading day (or by event date for the daily studies) unless stated. "bp" is basis points of the underlying's price.

The two promotion rules every entry was judged against:

- `GATE-v2` (in force): data integrity on full chains including extreme-move days; bid/ask fills at measured NBBO with fees and official settlement; positive net expectancy with a day-clustered 95 % lower bound above zero, out of sample, after multiplicity accounting; stated loss under the worst historical intraday move and ±10 %/±15 % shocks with capital sized to it; ≥ 150 sessions **and** ≥ 7 calendar months of forward paper trading with live NBBO logging; signal lanes must also beat a matched random-entry baseline by more than one round trip, carry lanes need not but must be labelled carry. Win rate is reported, never a gate.
- Protocol amendment A1 (underlying-level hurdle for an intraday trigger to earn an option test): absolute signed forward return with a day-clustered lower bound ≥ +3 bp at 30 or 60 minutes **and** a positive paired lower bound against the matched random control. A2/A3 (daily triggers): absolute lower bound ≥ +30 bp at 5, 10 or 21 sessions **and** benchmark-adjusted lower bound > 0. A5 batch: additionally the same sign in every year and survival of a Bonferroni-adjusted interval across the batch's 163 primary cells.

### 3.1 S13 pre-earnings run-up → option expression (A5.2), via the S11 pull

- **Evidence.** Underlying +97 bp absolute [+62, +133] over the 10 sessions before the report, adjusted +20 [+4, +36], same sign in 2024/2025/2026; the first and only cell to clear A2 on both parts. The option arithmetic in the registry says a 10-session long call cannot be carried by the drift alone; the pre-registered mechanism is the implied-volatility rise into the report.

- **Retail accessible:** yes (single long call / straddle / debit vertical at the NBBO, $0.70/side).
- **Testable with our data:** yes, as soon as the S11 pull finishes (pre_earnings group 1,199 events + 1,198 controls; 400/7,166 files at 21:13 UTC). ThetaData option EOD NBBO exists from 2012-06-04, so the window could extend to 2012 once the stock-EOD lake years 2012–2022 land (currently paused at 2022 part 003) and the earnings calendar (1996 →, already complete) is joined.

Expect the long-call cell to fail unless the IV-change measurement shows a rise of that order; the debit vertical caps theta but also caps the capture of the tail events that make the mean.
