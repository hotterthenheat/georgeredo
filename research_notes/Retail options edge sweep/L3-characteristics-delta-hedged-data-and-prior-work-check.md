# L3 — stock and option characteristics as predictors of delta-hedged single-name option returns: data and prior-work check (2026-10-14)

Lens: can the hypothesis actually be tested with the data in `02_coverage_matrix.md` over a window long enough for the gates in registry `GATE-v2` and `04_protocol.md`, and is it not already closed in `registry.jsonl`. Hypothesis under test: sweep id **L3** (`academic-anomalies.md` §3, §11) — "HV−IV long-short straddles 22.5 %/month gross falling to 7.5 % (draft) or 3.9 % (published) at the full quoted spread (Goyal–Saretto); delta-hedged returns fall monotonically in idiosyncratic volatility (Cao–Han); price level, profitability, issuance, dispersion predict returns to writing delta-hedged calls, 7 of 12 survive 100 % of quoted spread in the 2016 draft (Zhan et al.); ML ensemble 2.63 %/month at mid, 1.33 % at 15 % and 0.47 % at 25 % of quoted spread (Bali–Beckmeyer–Mörke–Weigert). ## 0. Verdict

**Refuted.** Not because the registry closes it (it does not; §1), and not on the evidence quality of the published effects (the sibling note handles that; the gross sorts are real), but on four data-and-window facts:

1. **The "smoke test" the hypothesis names cannot compute its own signal.** The S03 cache holds, per session, exactly one nearest-Friday expiration at 1, 2, 3, 4 or 7 days to expiry (SPY 98/96/85/97/92 session files by DTE; QQQ and IWM 57/59/63/58/57), on three index ETFs, on a year-stratified sample of sessions (SPY 468, QQQ 294, IWM 294 of 2,353). It contains no one-month at-the-money implied volatility, no second expiration for a term slope, and no cross-section. What it can do is a time-series sort of one ETF's ≤ 7-DTE implied-versus-realized ratio, which is the design of registry S15 on 0DTE (rich implied → the *larger* realized move, −$41.10 [−86.33, −2.39] for the short straddle), not Goyal–Saretto's cross-sectional one-month sort. §3.
2. **No cache on disk holds the inputs for the named characteristics, and two of them are not in any dataset we have.** The IV term slope needs two expirations per root-date; the S11 event cache (435 roots, 1,051 files at 22:25 UTC) has one 30–45-DTE expiration per root-date in every file (1,052 root-dates, 0 with a second expiration), the S03 cache one weekly, the 0DTE cache the same-day expiration only. Share issuance, profitability and analyst dispersion need Compustat/IBES-type fields that exist nowhere in the lake (the Unusual Whales earnings file carries reported and estimated EPS and the surprise, not analyst-level estimates). What is computable today — IV−HV at the close, price level, a CAPM-residual IVOL — is computable only for 2024-01 → 2026-09 on event-selected root-dates and their random-date controls, at closing marks the hypothesis's own source (Goyenko–Zhang) says are biased, on names whose closing near-ATM half-spread is 11.1 % median (`execution-costs.md` §2.2). §3.
3. **The real test is a pull that has not been made, on a universe that does not exist point in time, and the window it would yield is marginal for the one characteristic that matters and hopeless for the others.** A Tier A monthly cross-section 2012-06 → 2026-09 is ≈ 11,868 root-months for the 69 Tier A-2026 equities; at the measured vendor rates (option EOD 0.174 requests/s from the S11 pull; OI 0.1/s; whole-chain 1-minute quotes 0.2/s) two expirations of EOD plus two of 10:00 chains plus OI is ≈ 4.3 days of the single login (5.0 for the 80-equity 2025–2026 union; ≈ 88 days at the papers' ≈ 1,400-name breadth), on a login that today runs the S11 remainder and has the 2022 → 2012 stock-EOD backfill paused. Point-in-time tiers exist for 2025 and 2026 only; 19 of the 69 Tier A-2026 equities had no listed expiration by 2012-07-01 and 14 had none by 2017-01-01. The window gives 172 third-Friday formation dates, 170 holding months and 140 out-of-sample months under the protocol's calendar-year folds; at 14 names per quintile the gross IV−HV spread (−10.3 %/month in Heston et al.'s 1996–2019 quintiles) needs ≈ 164 months for a one-sided 95 % lower bound above zero, and the IVOL spread (−2.5 %/month) ≈ 2,800 months. §3, §4.
4. **As a filter it has nothing to filter, so its benefit cannot be measured against any gate.** Every Compass long-option lane in the registry is on index ETFs or is zero/negative (S01, S02, S03, S09, S12b, S16b), and the only single-name long-option cell is S11's pre-earnings group at n 18–20 fills on 12 % of the chains, ≈ 160 at the full pull, ≈ 32 per quintile. At the per-trade dispersion of a single-name option (80.6 %/month, ≈ 30 % over ≤ 3 sessions, ≈ 7 % over 60 minutes) and the effect sizes the ex-rollover literature supports (≈ 5 % of premium per month for the expensive-IV decile in the broad sample, ≈ 0.2 % in S&P 500 names, ≈ 0 for IVOL and price), the filter's benefit on a Compass hold needs ≈ 1,100–10,000 trades per quintile cell at ≤ 3 sessions and ≈ 20,000 at 60 minutes. GATE-v2's forward gate (≥ 150 sessions and ≥ 7 months of paper trading with live NBBO logging) has no lane to run on. §4.

None of this is a cost argument; the sibling note has that. The binding constraints here are that the signal cannot be formed from what is cached, the universe and three of the five characteristics are missing, the pull is days of the single login for a window that resolves only the strongest gross sort, and the filter's value is unmeasurable without a lane that does not exist. What could be registered — a gross, diagnostic Goyal–Saretto replication on Tier A at 10:00 marks — is stated in §6 with its cost and its answerable questions, none of which is "is there an edge".

## 2. Prior-work check: what each construction needs from data, and what exists after the papers' samples

### 2.1 Data requirements of the constructions as published (read for what a replication must hold)

- **Goyal & Saretto, *JFE* 94(2) 2009 [PR]** (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304405X09001251 ; abstract via https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v94y2009i2p310-326.html ; March 2008 draft http://www.ruf.rice.edu/~jgsfss/goyal_041808.pdf [WP]): OptionMetrics closing bid–ask 1996–2005; HV = standard deviation of daily returns over the trailing **twelve months** (not 21 days as the hypothesis's `data_needed` says); IV = average of the one-month ATM call and put IVs on the formation day; deciles; straddles and delta-hedged calls/puts bought at the closing midpoint and held to expiration (static hedge); formation on the Monday after expiration, trade on Tuesday's close (draft fn. 22). Inputs per root-month: the one-month ATM pair's closing NBBO and OI on one day, the stock's split-adjusted close at expiry, twelve months of daily stock closes. The abstract contains no numbers; the "3.9 % per month at the quoted spread" in the hypothesis text was not found in any primary or mirror text today either (the SUFE mirror https://academicnewsletter.sufe.edu.cn/info/357238 and IDEAS carry the number-free abstract; the Purdue CIBER WP 55 and ERIM copies are the 2007–2008 drafts) — it stays **unverified** and should be dropped from the hypothesis text.
- **Cao & Han, *JFE* 108(1) 2013 [PR]** (https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v108y2013i1p231-249.html ; text not retrieved, Rotman mirror 404 / SSRN 403): daily-rebalanced delta-hedged gains on ≈ 1.5-month ATM options scaled by (ΔS − C); idiosyncratic volatility = standard deviation of the **Fama–French three-factor residual** over the prior month's daily returns. Inputs: daily option closes or NBBO mids for every day of the hold (not just formation and expiry), daily deltas, and the FF3 factor series — the factors are free (Kenneth French data library) but are not in the lake; a CAPM residual against SPY is the only in-lake substitute.
- **Zhan, Han, Cao & Tong, *RFS* 35(3) 2022 [PR]** (https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/rfinst/v35y2022i3p1394-1442..html ; April 2016 draft https://abfer.org/media/abfer-events-2016/annual-conference/investment-finance/226-option-return-predictability.pdf [WP]; February 2016 draft https://www.mcgill.ca/desautels/files/desautels/option_return_predictability_0.pdf): 204 months 1996-01 → 2012-12, 792 stocks/month, options ≈ 50 calendar days to maturity at the month-end formation, gain to maturity per dollar of (ΔS − C); characteristics from **CRSP/Compustat/IBES** — cash holdings, cash-flow variance, share issuance, external financing, profit margin, profitability, distress (Z-score), analyst dispersion, size, price. Of these, only price (and size as market cap, which needs shares outstanding — not in the stock EOD schema) is in our lake. The draft's cost table (Table 8) carries **eight** strategies and "seven out of eight" survive 100 % of the quoted spread (the lone failure is size); the "seven of twelve" in the hypothesis is a misreading of the draft's skewness sentence, as the sibling note established.
- **Bali, Beckmeyer, Mörke & Weigert, *RFS* 36(9) 2023 [PR]** (https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/36/9/3548/7008024 ; CFR WP 21-08 https://www.cfr-cologne.de/download/workingpaper/cfr-21-08.pdf [WP]): 12.1 M option-month observations 1996-01 → 2020-12, 1,747 stocks/month, daily-rebalanced delta-hedged gains, 270 predictors from OptionMetrics, CRSP, Compustat and IBES; training 1996–2002, annual re-estimation 2003–2020. Nothing reported at 100 % of the quoted spread; at 50 % "the unconditional strategy fails to generate positive returns"; the extreme deciles have the widest relative spreads (0.20 low, 0.15 high). Irreproducible on our lake for lack of the fundamental and analyst blocks (roughly half the 270 predictors).
- **Vasquez, *JFQA* 52(6) 2017 [PR]** (https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/equity-volatility-term-structures-and-the-cross-section-of-option-returns/F0A40E99FD2458367DD9A56A89783D38 ; abstract only): the slope of the implied-volatility term structure needs **two expirations per root-date** (the one-month ATM IV and a longer-dated ATM IV); straddle portfolios held one month. No cache on disk has two expirations for any root-date (§3).
- **Heston, Jones, Khorram, Li & Mo, *JF* 78(6) 2023 [PR]** (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13279 ; September 2021 draft https://www.acem.sjtu.edu.cn/sffs/2021w/pdfs/3.pdf [WP]): the 1996–2019 quintile replication of all four sorts that the hypothesis quotes (IV−HV high−low −10.34 %/month, t −9.48; IVOL −2.49, t −1.99; IV term spread +7.17, t 7.92; every long-straddle cell negative). Same construction as L2 (`L2-option-momentum-data-and-prior-work-check.md` §2.1): common equity on the regular monthly cycle, ATM pair by OptionMetrics delta, positive OI at selection, payoff from the CRSP split-adjusted close at expiry. The data items that note found missing for L2 — point-in-time universe, CTA-tape payoff series before 2017, corporate-action layer, delisting returns — are the same items for L3.

### 2.2 Evidence after the papers' samples, and what was not found

- **The most recent sample is August 2023** (Garcia-Ares & Muravyev, FMA 2025 [WP], https://www.fma.org/assets/docs/Derivatives2025/GarciaAres.pdf , OptionMetrics 1996-01 → 2023-08, read by the sibling note): 18 anomalies at −0.64 %/month of hedged capital on average, of which −0.36 % falls on the third-Friday rollover and the following Monday; in S&P 500 names the ex-rollover average is +0.04 % (t 0.68). This is the only decomposition in time and the only post-2020 sample; it is monthly, delta-hedged, closing marks, OptionMetrics.
- **Käfer, Mörke & Wiest, *JFQA* 61(1) 2026 [PR]** (https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022109025000225): OptionMetrics to 2021-12, daily delta-hedged calls — option *factor* momentum, not the characteristic sorts; cited by the L2 data note; it confirms the data pipeline (filters at initiation only, intrinsic value when no closing price) and nothing about L3's sorts post-2019.
- **Neuhierl, Tang, Varneskov & Zhou, "Option Characteristics as Cross-Sectional Predictors" (SSRN 3795486 [WP]; published as "Do Option Characteristics Predict the Underlying Stock Returns in the Cross-Section?", *Management Science* 2025, DOI 10.1287/mnsc.2024.04720 [PR])** — https://publikationen.ub.uni-frankfurt.de/opus4/files/65244/SSRN-id3795486.pdf ; https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/suppl/10.1287/mnsc.2024.04720 (replication files listed). Searched for as a possible post-2020 characteristic-sort replication: it is the reverse direction (option characteristics predicting **stock** returns), i.e. the L5 family, not L3; sample period and cost treatment not retrieved. Not evidence for or against L3.
- **Not found after three searches (two extended):** (i) any test of these characteristic sorts on *weekly* or ≤ 7-DTE single-name options, or on holding periods of minutes to three sessions — the closest is Cao, Vasquez, Xiao & Zhan's robustness line that their volatility-of-volatility effect holds "whether the delta-hedged option return is held until week end, month end or maturity" (https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2020/preliminary/paper/k6denSQi [WP]), which is a one-week hold of a ≈ 50-DTE option, not a weekly option; (ii) any replication on non-OptionMetrics data (ThetaData, OPRA-derived, Polygon) or at intraday quotes; (iii) any post-publication decay study for option-return anomalies — the decay literature returned by the searches (McLean–Pontiff; Hou–Xue–Zhang https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w23394/w23394.pdf ; Chen–Velikov https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/945133D5A3ECEEAF466AEE91551FD225/S0022109022000874a.pdf/zeroing-in-on-the-expected-returns-of-anomalies.pdf [PR]) is about stocks; (iv) any after-cost column at 100 % of the quoted spread on data after 2012 by a third party. The Erasmus thesis on adaptive group LASSO factors (https://thesis.eur.nl/pub/67226/ [SEC; student thesis, not evidence]) and the PapersWithBacktest page on Cao–Han (https://paperswithbacktest.com/strategies/cross-section-of-option-returns-and-idiosyncratic-stock-volatility [VM; vendor catalogue, not evidence]) are listed only to flag them.

Reading for the data check: every published construction is monthly, uses OptionMetrics closing quotes and CRSP/Compustat/IBES, holds one to two months, and measures delta-hedged or straddle returns — none measures the quantity Compass would use (the raw return of a long single-name weekly or 0DTE option over minutes to three sessions). There is no prior work at that tenor to calibrate an expected effect size against; the sibling note's scaling (vol-carry content ∝ fraction of the option's life held) is the only available estimate, and it is an assumption.

## 4. Window length against the gates [LOCAL, `scripts_l3/l3_data_check.py`]

- **Holding months.** Third-Friday formation dates 2012-06-15 → 2026-09-18: **172**; with a 21-session realized-volatility warm-up from the 2012-06-01 stock floor the first formation is 2012-07-20 → **170 holding months** (Goyal–Saretto's twelve-month HV would push the first formation to 2013-06 → 159, the L2 count). `04_protocol.md` §6: calendar-year folds, first test year = first year with two full prior years of option quotes → 2015 → **140 out-of-sample months**. Protocol §7 minimum evidence is "≥ 150 out-of-sample trades over ≥ 3 months per reported cell": if the unit is the monthly portfolio return (the only unit with independent observations in a cross-sectional sort — every straddle in a month shares the holding window, so day-clustering collapses to month-clustering), 140 < 150, as for L1 and L2. Counting contracts satisfies the letter, not the purpose.
- **Power for the gross sort** (one-sided 95 % lower bound > 0, n = (1.645 σ / effect)²). At the papers' breadth (≈ 270 names per quintile, long-short SD ≈ 18 %/month from Heston's t −9.48 over 282 months): IV−HV at 10.3 %/month needs **9 months**; IVOL at 2.5 % needs **141** — i.e. the paper-breadth pull of §3 item 9 (≈ 88 login-days) would resolve both inside 140 OOS months, barely for IVOL. At 30 names per quintile (≈ 150 roots; idiosyncratic SD ≈ 55 % for the long-short, using Heston's single-straddle SD of 80.6 %): **78 months** for IV−HV, **1,310** for IVOL. At **14 names per quintile** (the 69 Tier A-2026 equities; SD ≈ 80 %): **164 months** for IV−HV against 170 holding / 140 OOS, **≈ 2,800** for IVOL. The price-level sort is weaker still ex-rollover (−0.15 %/month of hedged capital, t −1.07, Garcia-Ares–Muravyev Table 4). So on Tier A the window resolves the gross IV−HV sort marginally and in-sample only, and nothing else; and the gross sort is not the hypothesis — the hypothesis is the filter's benefit on a long-option hold.
- **Power for the filter on a Compass lane.** Per-trade SD of a single-name option: 80.6 %/month (Heston Table 1); scaled by √(fraction of the month held): **≈ 30.5 % over ≤ 3 sessions**, **≈ 6.9 % over 60 minutes**. The vol-carry content of a raw long-option P&L scales with the same fraction: ≈ 5 % of premium per month for the expensive-IV decile in the broad sample (Garcia-Ares–Muravyev decile 10 ex-rollover −0.55 % of hedged capital; sibling §3), ≈ 0.2 % in S&P 500 names, ≈ 0 for IVOL and price ex-rollover → ≈ 0.7 % / 0.03 % per ≤ 3-session trade, ≈ 0.08 % / 0.003 % per 60-minute trade. Trades per quintile cell for a positive lower bound: **1,117 at 1.5 %, 10,046 at 0.5 %, 251,000 at 0.1 %** over ≤ 3 sessions; **≈ 20,000 at 0.08 %** over 60 minutes; at a one-month hold **704 at 5 %, 440,000 at 0.2 %**. The only single-name long-option cell in the registry will have ≈ 32 trades per quintile at the full S11 pull (§3 item 12). The S03 cache's 818 fills are on three index ETFs, where the cross-sectional characteristic is undefined.
- **Forward validation (GATE-v2: ≥ 150 sessions and ≥ 7 calendar months of paper trading with live NBBO logging).** A filter is validated by the lane it filters; there is no single-name long-option lane in paper trading, and the pre-registered candidate (A5.2 via S11) is expected to fail on S13-R's own reading. Seven months of a monthly cross-sectional sort is seven observations of an 18–80 %-SD series.
- **Multiplicity (A5 batch rule).** Five characteristics × quintile-exclusion thresholds × two return definitions (delta-hedged, raw) × hold lengths; promotion of a follow-up cell "requires the same sign in every year of its window and survival of a Bonferroni-adjusted interval across the batch". At 14 names per quintile a single year of twelve months has a standard error of ≈ 23 % on the long-short against a mean of ≈ 10 % for the strongest sort; a same-sign-every-year requirement is unattainable at Tier A breadth even for IV−HV.
- **Data integrity (GATE-v2: full chains incl. extreme-move days, hashed).** The S03 cache is a stratified session sample and S11 is event-selected; a monthly replication would need its own complete per-expiration chains per formation month, pulled and hashed — that is the §3 item 9 pull, not anything on disk.

## 5. Retail accessibility (brief; the sibling note has the detail)

The filter is retail-accessible at zero execution cost: it only removes candidates. The profitable leg of every source (delta-hedged or naked single-name call/straddle writing on ≈ 80 names per decile, rebalanced monthly, with the profit concentrated on two rollover days in the most recent decomposition) is outside the Compass long-option universe (H15 scope note) and outside A4's defined-risk opening, needs the highest option approval tier and Reg-T margin of ≈ 3.5 × the straddle's value for a one-month ATM single-name straddle (FINRA 4210(f)(2)(E); `execution-costs.md` §7), and is priced in the papers at execution-timing effective spreads a marketable order does not get. The long leg — the only expression in scope — is negative gross in every published cell. Nothing in the data check changes that classification.

## 6. What could be registered instead (for the owner's scope decision; not a recommendation), and what should not

1. **A gross diagnostic replication of the Goyal–Saretto sort on Tier A, labelled data-quality.** The 52 Tier A-2026 equities with a listed expiration by 2013-01-01 (to limit survivorship until point-in-time tiers exist), monthly one-month ATM straddle at the 10:00 1-minute NBBO mid on the formation day *and* at the closing EOD mid, OI at selection, held to expiry with the payoff from stock EOD where the tape floor allows (UTP names 2012-06 →, CTA names 2017-01 → as measured) and from the expiring chain's intrinsic elsewhere, flagged; a second expiration per root-month for the term slope; quintile sorts on IV−HV (12-month and 21-session HV both), CAPM-IVOL, price, term slope; month-clustered CIs. Pull ≈ 4.3 login-days (§3 item 9). It would answer three questions no published source answers — whether the one-month sort survives 10:00 marks, whether it held 2019-07 → 2026-09 on liquid names (Garcia-Ares–Muravyev's S&P 500 ex-rollover +0.04 % says no), and what single-name half-spreads were on 2012–2024 chains (item 10) — and it would be the first replication on non-OptionMetrics data. It cannot answer the after-cost or the filter question (§4), and at Tier A breadth it resolves only the IV−HV sort and only in-sample. Pre-register it as a diagnostic with no promotion path, or do not run it.
2. **The cheap check that is possible now, on the S11 controls.** The 1,789 random-date control root-dates (`s11_events.parquet`: breakout20_control 591, beat_top_tercile_control 598, pre_earnings_control 600) are an unselected sample of the S07 universe 2024–2026 with one 30–45-DTE closing chain each. IV−HV, price and CAPM-IVOL can be computed on them and the closing-NBBO raw and delta-hedged return of the ATM pair measured to 21 sessions — one afternoon of engine time, no pull. Expected outcome: 33 holding months, ≈ 54 root-dates per month, closing marks, 11.1 % median half-spreads — a sign check on the IV−HV sort at the close in 2024–2026, nothing more; it cannot be gated (closing execution is ruled out for single names by `execution-costs.md` §2.2) and it is three years of one regime. Worth running only to decide whether item 1 is worth its login-days.
3. **The exclusion rule** already stated by the sibling note (§5): if Compass ever holds a single-name long option, exclude the top quintile of one-month ATM IV minus trailing realized volatility at decision time, and treat IVOL and price level as covered by L4's lottery exclusion. Adopt by default like H14/H17; do not register it as a lane or claim a size for it — the data cannot measure one (§4).
4. **Should not be registered:** L3 as a lane in either direction; the "S03 smoke test" (it cannot form the signal and would reproduce S15's design on weeklies); the term-slope, issuance, dispersion and profitability characteristics (no data); any filter benefit claim on a Compass hold (no lane; 1,100–20,000 trades per cell needed at the supportable effect sizes). Re-propose only with (a) point-in-time tiers for 2013–2024 from the landed daily bars, (b) a measured single-name half-spread table on 2012–2024 chains, (c) a single-name long-option lane with ≥ 1,000 fills per quintile cell, and (d) a published test at the weekly or intraday tenor — none of which exists today.
