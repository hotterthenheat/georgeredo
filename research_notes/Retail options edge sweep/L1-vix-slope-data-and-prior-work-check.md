# L1 — VIX term-structure slope as a timing signal for 30-DTE SPY/SPXW straddles: data and prior-work check (2026-10-14)

Lens: can the hypothesis actually be tested with the data in `02_coverage_matrix.md` over a window long enough for the gates in registry `GATE-v2` and `04_protocol.md`, and is it not already closed in `registry.jsonl`. Hypothesis under test: sweep id **L1** (`academic-anomalies.md` §1.2) — the VIX term-structure slope (Johnson 2017's second principal component, SLOPE) predicts S&P 500 straddle returns at all maturities, low slope → high subsequent variance-asset returns; test the slope, not the level, at the 30-DTE tenor on SPY (and SPXW), monthly at 10:30, 21-session holds, long and short, cells by quintile of VIX3M − VIX9D and of VIX minus 21-day realized, 2012-06 → 2026-10, fills at the 1-minute NBBO with C1 costs. Proposition to refute: *a retail-accessible option edge that is plausibly positive after costs and testable with our data over a window long enough for our gates.*

Grades: **[PR]** peer-reviewed; **[WP]** working paper; **[EX]** exchange/regulator publication or data; **[AUTH-DATA]** replication data published by the author of a peer-reviewed paper (graded with the paper, but the post-sample extension in it is unpublished); **[SEC]** secondary summary or search-engine summary of a source not read (not evidence); **[LOCAL]** this repository's measurements or this note's own computation. Local context read first: `research/compass/02_coverage_matrix.md`, `07_coverage_report.md`, `05_results.md`, `04_protocol.md` (§6, §7, §10, amendments A1–A5.2), `registry.jsonl` (45 entries), `research/compass/data_audit/probe1.log`, `probe2.log`, `thetadata_boundaries*.json`, `data_lake/universe/expirations.jsonl.gz`, and the sibling notes `academic-anomalies.md`, `execution-costs.md`, `our-evidence-ledger.md`, `vrp-carry-practitioners.md`, `L1-vix-term-structure-slope-refutation.md` (evidence-quality lens) and `L1-vix-slope-straddle-cost-realism.md` (cost lens). Nothing below repeats those two L1 notes' proxy screens; it adds what they did not have — the author's own post-2013 data, the proxy-versus-SLOPE check, the vendor probe record for the index legs, and the gate arithmetic.

## 0. Verdict

**Refuted.** Not because the registry closes it (it does not, §1) and not because the data are absent (the SPY leg is in the vendor floor, §3), but because:

1. **The paper's own data, extended by its author to 2019-06-28 and downloadable today, show the long low-slope straddle cell gone after 2013.** With Johnson's published principal components and daily 1-month straddle returns (`vixts_pcs.csv`, `straddle_ret.csv`, 1996-01-04 → 2019-06-28 [AUTH-DATA]) I reproduce his Table 5 Panel C to the second decimal on 1996–2013 (lowest-SLOPE quintile **+0.44 % per day**, High − Low **−1.08 %**; paper −1.09). On 2014-01 → 2019-06 (1,380 sessions, out of the paper's sample) the lowest-SLOPE quintile earns **+0.09 % per day (SE 0.54)** next day and **−4.6 % of premium [−15.8, +8.9] over 21 sessions**; on 2012-06 → 2019-06 (the hypothesis's own window, as far as the author's data go) **−5.4 % [−15.2, +7.4]** over 21 sessions, negative in six of seven calendar years; on monthly entries (first session on or after the 15th, n = 84) the lowest-SLOPE cell is **−3.9 % [−18.5, +14.4] (n 17)**. These are daily-rebalanced constant-maturity straddles at closing midpoints with no costs — the measurement that Goyenko & Zhang show is biased *upward* — so the long cell is overstated, not understated, here. What the slope still sorts after 2013 is the size of the *short* carry (steepest quintile +2.2 % of 20 %-margin per 21 sessions [+1.07, +3.30] versus +0.96 % [+0.19, +1.66] unconditional), which is the carry lane outside the Compass long-option scope (`H15` scope note; `08_port_spec` §5). §2.
2. **The conditioning variable the hypothesis proposes is not the paper's variable.** VIX3M − VIX9D has a correlation of **+0.40** with Johnson's SLOPE over their 2,135 overlapping sessions (2011-01-04 → 2019-06-28); only 38 % of SLOPE's bottom-quintile days are also the proxy's bottom-quintile days; the proxy is −0.56 correlated with the VIX level while SLOPE is orthogonal to the level by construction (PC2 vs PC1). The "slope" cells of the suggested test are therefore mostly high-VIX cells — the level sort that S15 found had the wrong sign at 0DTE and that Johnson's Table 7 controls away. The best two-point free proxy, VIX6M − VIX (+0.52 with SLOPE), gives a bottom-quintile long cell of **+0.2 % [−12.1, +17.2]** over 21 sessions on 2011–2019: zero. §2.3.
3. **The window cannot satisfy the gates for any conditional cell, and the SPXW leg is not in verified data.** 172 monthly cycles 2012-06 → 2026-09 (171 with a 21-session exit); the protocol's calendar-year walk-forward with two full prior years of option quotes makes 2015 the first out-of-sample year, leaving **141** out-of-sample monthly trades unconditionally (below the §7 minimum of 150 per reported cell) and ≈ 28 per quintile; the forward-paper gate for a long-option lane (≥ 150 trades, §6.4) is **12.5 years** at monthly cadence. SPX and SPXW option-quote floors were never verified (the `option_list_dates` probes for both roots errored in `boundary_probe.py`); SPXW had **no third-Friday expiration before 2017** (third-Friday PM-settled SPX traded under the separate `SPXPM` root, 66 expirations 2012-06-16 → 2018-12-21 in our expirations file, moved into SPXW by SEC Release 34-80060), and its 2012–2016 weeklies put the nearest ≥ 28-DTE contract at 28–41 DTE in a listing regime where weeklies were quoted only about four weeks ahead. §3, §4.

Cost is not the binding constraint (the cost lens already showed the 2.1 %-of-premium round trip is one to two orders of magnitude below the monthly dispersion); the binding constraints are the sign of the retail-accessible cell on every dataset that reaches past 2013, the mismatch between the proposed sort and the published factor, and the sample size the gates require. Nothing in L1 as written should be registered as an experiment; what *could* be registered is in §6.

Reading. (i) The paper replicates exactly on its own files. (ii) The economically interesting cell for a long-option program — positive expected straddle returns when the slope is low — is **a 1996–2013 phenomenon on these data**: its next-day mean falls from +0.44 % to +0.09 % with a standard error that doubles, its 21-session mean is negative in every post-2013 cut, and under the paper's own expanding-quintile strategy the "buy" state is reached on 24 of 1,380 post-2013 sessions (the term structure after 2013 rarely reaches the 1996–2013 bottom quintile, so the rule would have been short almost every day). The hypothesis's sort shares 16 % of its variance with the published factor and is first a VIX-level sort (−0.56), which is exactly the dimension Johnson removes (PC2 ⟂ PC1) and controls for in Table 7, and the dimension whose 0DTE version S15 found wrong-signed. Johnson's own robustness row (Table 6: the two-point slope VIX²_12 − VIX²_1 gives −0.57 % per day versus −1.09 % for SLOPE, "statistically significant but muted") already said that a two-point slope is a weaker instrument; a two-point slope at the *short* end (9 days to 3 months) is weaker still on these numbers. A test of "the slope, not the level" needs SLOPE — which is free through 2019-06 from the author and would have to be re-estimated from a multi-tenor term structure after that (Cboe's VIX9D, VIX, VIX3M, VIX6M, and VIX1Y if its history is published — not verified here); ThetaData holds no SPX option chain before 2022-05 from which to compute VIX²_K ourselves (§3).

## 4. Window length against the gates

- `04_protocol.md` §6: folds are calendar years; the first test year is the first with at least two full prior years of option quotes in the family's category → 2015. Out-of-sample monthly trades 2015-01 → 2026-09: **141** unconditional (< 150, the §7 "minimum evidence … ≥ 150 out-of-sample trades over ≥ 3 months per reported cell"); per slope quintile ≈ 28; per cell of the suggested 2 roots × 2 structures × 2 sides × 2 sorts × 5 quintiles design, 28 at best, marked "underpowered" by the protocol's own rule. Johnson's data say the same thing differently: the paper's in-sample Q1 21-session cell, n 906 overlapping days, has an interval of [−1.9, +14.2] that already includes zero.
- `GATE-v2` / §6.4: forward paper ≥ 150 trades and ≥ 3 months for a long-option lane → 12.5 years at monthly cadence; for a carry lane ≥ 150 sessions and ≥ 7 calendar months → 7 straddles.
- A5 batch rule (same sign in every year plus a Bonferroni-adjusted interval across the batch): the lowest-SLOPE long cell is negative in 6 of 7 years on Johnson's data (2013–2019) and in 11 of 13 / 13 of 15 years on the sibling notes' Cboe proxies (2012–2026).
- Daily overlapping entries (≈ 3,590) do not change this: they are 171 independent 21-session blocks, and the protocol's day-clustered bootstrap would understate the overlap; intervals must be clustered by month-block (as in §2.2 and in both sibling notes).
- The previously-analysed-window rule (§6.3) does not bite here, but every historical session 2012 → 2026 has now been used by three notes' proxy screens; a chain-based re-run of the same cells would be confirmatory, not out of sample.

## 5. Retail accessibility (unchanged from the sibling notes; stated for completeness)

Long straddle: two marketable orders at the NBBO, $0.70 per contract per side, no margin — fully retail-accessible, and it is the side with no positive cell on any dataset. Short straddle: naked index straddle margin (greater of the put or call requirement plus the other premium; ≈ $16–17 k per SPY straddle at SPY 777, ≈ $100 k on SPX; `execution-costs.md` §7), highest options approval, and a 2020-02-type month of several multiples of premium; the H15 capital profile. Not institutional-only, not a small-account edge, and outside scope until the owner opens short premium.

## 6. What could be registered instead (for the owner's scope decision; not a recommendation)

1. **If short premium is placed in scope** — a carry-lane hypothesis distinct from L1, as both sibling notes already state: 30-DTE SPY ATM short straddle or ±0.5×EM strangle, monthly at 10:30, held to expiry (H14 exit rule), only in the steepest quintile of a SLOPE-faithful variable, skip FOMC/month-end per H17, priced at a measured 30-DTE NBBO (one pass over the pulled chains), stressed under GATE-v2 (±10 %/±15 % and the 2020-02 path), labelled carry, forward paper ≥ 150 sessions / 7 months. Expected from §2.2 and the sibling proxies: +11 % (Johnson 2014–2019, margin-based) to +30–44 % (Cboe proxy 2012–2026) of premium per qualifying month gross, ≈ 2 points less after costs, with the conditional improvement over the unconditional short resting on 28–35 months and on 2020–2021.
2. **The conditioning variable must be SLOPE, not VIX3M − VIX9D.** Johnson's `vixts_pcs.csv` supplies it free through 2019-06-28; after that it has to be re-estimated from a multi-tenor Cboe term structure or from SPX chains, which ThetaData holds only from 2022-05. A pre-registered test should state the variable and its source before any chain is pulled.
3. **The long cells of L1 should not be run.** Three independent datasets (the author's straddle returns to 2019; two Cboe-based proxies to 2026) and the author's own OOS design agree that the low-slope long straddle has no positive expectation after 2013 before costs; no chain pull can change a sign that is set by the realized-versus-implied variance of the months the rule selects.
