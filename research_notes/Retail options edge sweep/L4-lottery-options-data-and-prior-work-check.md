# L4 — lottery-like options as a "retail exclusion rule": data and prior-work check (2026-10-14)

Lens: can the hypothesis actually be tested with the data in `02_coverage_matrix.md` over a window long enough for the gates in registry `GATE-v2` and `04_protocol.md`, and is it not already closed in `registry.jsonl`. Hypothesis under test: sweep id **L4** (`academic-anomalies.md` §4, §11) — "ex-ante total-skewness quintile spreads of 10–50 % per week (Boyer–Vorkink), calls on the most lottery-like stocks underperform by 10–20 % per month (Byun–Kim), risk-neutral skewness negatively priced (Bali–Murray); retail is the buyer of exactly these contracts and loses (Bryzgalova et al.; Beckmeyer et al.; de Silva–Smith–So). Retail-accessible expression is a hard exclusion rule, not a lane." Suggested test as stated: drop contracts with ask < $0.10 or premium < 1 % of spot for ≥ 5-day expirations, top-quintile smirk (OTM-put IV − ATM-call IV), top-quintile MAX or IVOL; "measure the gross return spread between excluded and retained contracts on the S03 cache and Tier A chains at NBBO; adopt as a default rule (like H14/H17) if the excluded set underperforms with a positive lower bound"; data needed "stock EOD (MAX, IVOL) from 2012-06/2017-01; option chains for the smirk and premium/spot; already required by S07/S11"; window "2012-06 to 2026-10 (stock EOD years still landing 2022 to 2012)"; expected size "zero cost … does not create a positive lane". Proposition to refute: *a retail-accessible option edge that is plausibly positive after costs and testable with our data over a window long enough for our gates.*

## 0. Verdict

**Refuted — and, on this lens, not because the hypothesis is false but because the test it names cannot be run on our data in any form that a gate could read, and the part of it that can be run is already in force.** Five data-and-window findings:

1. **The excluded set cannot be priced at the NBBO, so "the gross return spread between excluded and retained contracts at NBBO" is undefined for most of it.** At the 10:00 1-minute NBBO on the cached chains [LOCAL, stratified sample of 40 sessions per product], the share of quoted contracts with ask < $0.10 is 46 % (SPY 0DTE), 44 % (QQQ 0DTE), 35 % (SPXW 0DTE), 38 % / 34 % / 38 % (SPY / QQQ / IWM weeklies); **66–100 % of those contracts have a zero bid**, and the median half-spread of the sub-$0.10 set is 100 % of the mid on every product. A contract with no bid has a sell-side return of −100 % by construction and a midpoint return that Amaya et al. and Duarte–Jones–Wang show is biased upward. The only measurable "spread" on that wing is the Coval–Shumway moneyness pattern at midpoints, which is what Boyer–Vorkink measured (their IA Table IA.II: 92–98 % of the ex-ante skewness rank is moneyness and volatility) and which no NBBO fill can realise. §3 items 1–2.
2. **The ask floor excludes nothing Compass fills; the premium/spot floor excludes most of what it fills, in the wrong direction.** Zero of the 2,886 S01 fills, 1,229 S09 fills and 818 S03 fills have an entry ask below $0.10 (S03 minimum $0.24; S01 median $0.815; the contract rule already requires ask ≥ $0.05 and delta 0.30–0.60). "Premium < 1 % of spot for ≥ 5-day expirations" removes 80.1 % of all S03 fills (SPY 85 %, QQQ 79 %, IWM 73 %; 91 % at 1 DTE, 58 % at 7 DTE) and, on the only ≥ 5-DTE fills that exist (161, all at 7 DTE), 93 of 161; the 93 excluded earned +$4.6 per contract (sd $52) against +$27.5 (sd $104) for the 68 retained — a difference with a standard error of about $13, inside noise and with the sign the hypothesis predicts, but produced by IV × √T, not by lotteryness (on the single-name S11 cache the same rule binds on 2 % of root-dates: a 30–45-DTE ATM call is 3.4–6.6 % of spot). §3 items 3–4.
3. **The cross-sectional filters have no cross-section to act on.** MAX, IVOL and the smirk are stock-level ranks; every long-option trade set in the registry is on three index ETFs or SPXW (S01, S02, S03, S09, S12b, S16b), where "top quintile of MAX/IVOL" collapses into a time-series regime skip. S01 by VIX tercile is −2.18 [−4.10, −0.31] / −3.30 [−5.88, −0.80] / −7.18 [−10.04, −4.42]: the high-volatility tercile is the worst, and the other two are negative, so a regime exclusion cannot produce a positive cell (H17 found the same for carry: VIX-level skips lower the mean). The only single-name long-option fills in the registry are S11's pre-earnings call / straddle / debit-vertical cells at n ≤ 27 per structure (12 % of the chains), ≈ 160 at the full pull, ≈ 32 per quintile. §3 item 5, §4.
4. **The inputs the hypothesis says it has are vendor floors, not lake contents.** MAX and IVOL need daily stock bars: the lake holds **2023 → 2026** (four `year=` folders; 2022 "downloading" since 16:35 UTC and paused at part 003 at 20:33 for the S11 pull; 2012–2021 queued at lowest priority behind research pulls on the single login). The smirk needs a chain per name per day: the only single-name chains on disk are the S11 event cache — 1,166 files on 474 roots, **2024-01 → 2026-09, one 30–45-DTE expiration per root-date, closing NBBO only**, event-selected names plus 1,789 random-date controls — on which the smirk is formable for 89 % of root-dates (median three OTM puts at 0.80–0.95 moneyness with a bid) at closing marks on names whose closing near-ATM half-spread is 11.1 % median. Boyer–Vorkink's ex-ante skewness itself needs monthly cross-sectional regressions on turnover (volume ÷ shares outstanding), a NASDAQ exchange-code dummy, size terciles and 16 French industry dummies (IA §II, eq. IA.3–IA.4) — none of which is in any dataset we hold. Point-in-time tiers exist for 2025 and 2026 only; 50 of the 69 Tier A-2026 equities had a listed expiration by 2012-07-01. §3 items 6–9.
5. **No gate can read a rule that has no lane, and the H14 precedent the hypothesis invokes needs a trade set the data does not provide.** H14 was adopted on matched pairs in a TRAIN/TEST split (+$0.3 / +$2.7 per trade, positive in 89–95 % of pairs) applied to lanes that existed. L4's per-contract benefit inside the Compass band is bounded by the residual skewness effect once moneyness and volatility are fixed (Aretz–Lin–Poon: implied skewness t ≤ 1.85 beside moneyness t ≥ 5; Goyal–Saretto 2025: skewness characteristics' IPCA alphas insignificant), i.e. well under 1 % of premium per week. On the S03 fill set (per-contract P&L sd $62.4 on ≈ $500 of premium over ≤ 3 sessions) a $5 (1 % of premium) excluded-minus-retained difference needs ≈ 843 fills per group for a one-sided 95 % lower bound above zero, $2 needs ≈ 5,300, $1 needs ≈ 21,000; nine years of three ETFs produced 818 fills in total and 161 at ≥ 5 DTE. GATE-v2's forward gate (≥ 150 sessions and ≥ 7 months of paper trading with live NBBO) has nothing to run on. §4.

None of this is a cost argument or an evidence-quality argument; the sibling note has both (the sorts are moneyness sorts; retail loses at ATM, not on the lottery wing; the premium/spot rule is defective as drafted; the Amaya citation is wrong). On this lens the hypothesis's own `testable_with_our_data: true` is the error: the ask-floor half is unmeasurable at NBBO and already enforced; the premium/spot half is measurable only on index weeklies, where it is not a lottery filter and is inside noise at n 161; the stock-characteristic half needs a cross-section, a point-in-time universe, the 2012–2022 daily years and per-name chains that are not on disk. Expected size after costs: **zero**, as the hypothesis itself states; what it would avoid is already avoided by the delta band and the ask floor.

## 2. Prior-work check: what each construction needs from data, and what exists after the samples

### 2.1 Data requirements of the constructions as published

- **Boyer & Vorkink, *JF* 69(4) 2014 [PR]** — https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jfinan/v69y2014i4p1485-1527.html ; Internet Appendix https://boyer.byu.edu/00000180-910c-dc15-abb6-d9dfb7040001/boyer-vorkink-appendix-pdf (read; `l4_txt/bv_appendix.txt`). Ex-ante option skewness is built from the stock's *expected* physical skewness, forecast each month by a cross-sectional regression (IA eq. IA.3–IA.4, following Boyer–Mitton–Vorkink 2010) of realised daily-return skewness on lagged skewness, lagged volatility, momentum (t−13 → t−2), **turnover (daily volume ÷ shares outstanding)**, a **NASDAQ dummy (CRSP exchange code 3)**, size-tercile dummies and **16 French industry dummies**, then mapped to each contract's skewness "based on the lognormal assumption" with OptionMetrics deltas (IA §VI). Portfolios: seven-day options formed on the second Friday of each month, 18-day options on the first trading day; equal-weighted; midpoint returns held to expiration. Main text not retrieved (BYU Cloudflare / SSRN / Wiley); the 1996–2009 sample and the "up to 60 % per week" draft magnitudes come from CXO's summary [SEC] https://www.cxoadvisory.com/?p=13232 and the published abstract. Inputs we do not hold: shares outstanding, exchange code, industry classification, a CRSP-wide stock universe (the regression is estimated on all stocks, not on optionable names), and vendor deltas (the engine computes its own from the NBBO mid with r = 0.04, `engine/greeks.py`). The appendix's own decomposition (IA Table IA.II: moneyness 92–93 %, + volatility 97 %, all inputs 98 % of the rank's variance) means a replication that skips the regression and sorts on moneyness and realised volatility would reproduce ≈ 97 % of the ranking — which is the Compass delta band plus an IV sort.
- **Byun & Kim, *JFE* 122(1) 2016 [PR]** — https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v122y2016i1p155-174.html ; abstract only (ScienceDirect 403; KAIST record no file). Lottery-likeness of the *stock* (the sweep's "MAX, IVOL, price" reading follows Kumar 2009's lottery index but could not be verified in the text), raw call returns by month, put–call-parity deviations. Sample period, holding period and cost treatment unverified. What a replication needs at minimum: monthly stock-level MAX (largest daily return over the prior month), idiosyncratic volatility (a factor residual — the FF3 factors are free but not in the lake; a CAPM residual against SPY is the only in-lake substitute, and SPY's own daily bars start 2017-01 on this vendor), price level, and one-month ATM call returns per name per month.
- **Bali & Murray, *JFQA* 48(4) 2013 [PR]** — https://optionmetrics.com/research/t-g-bali-and-s-murrary-does-risk-neutral-skewness-predict-the-cross-section-of-equity-option-portfolio-returns ; supplement and Gruber's EFA 2011 discussion read by the sibling. Instrument: delta- and vega-neutral three-leg "skewness assets" on large caps, rebalanced monthly at midquotes, requiring vendor deltas and vegas across two strikes per name per month. Sample period not retrieved. Not a long-option exclusion criterion; its data needs (two strikes, Greeks, monthly rebalancing, margin) are those of an institutional skew trade.
- **Hu & Jacobs, *JFQA* 55(3) 2020 [PR]** — draft https://www.mcgill.ca/desautels/files/desautels/hj_jan29_2016.pdf (read; `l4_txt/hu_jacobs.txt` lines 423, 473, 566–567): OptionMetrics January 1996–July 2013, 247,859 calls / 188,046 puts, monthly expirations, moneyness 0.95–1.05, held to expiration from the midpoint, Panel C at the ask price "of a similar order". The raw-return volatility sort needs the underlying's 30-day realised volatility (daily bars) and monthly ATM contracts per name — the same per-name monthly pull as L2/L3.
- **The retail-side papers** (Beckmeyer et al. SSRN 4404704 [WP]; de Silva–Smith–So *RoF* [PR]; Bryzgalova–Pavlova–Sikorskaya *JF* 2023 [PR]; Amaya et al. 2025 [WP, Cboe-funded]) use exchange trader-type or auction flags (Cboe PIA/SLIM, Nasdaq NOTO/PHOTO) that no vendor we hold exposes; Unusual Whales flow is 2023-11 → and unsigned by customer type (DATA-2). "Retail is the buyer of exactly these contracts" is therefore untestable here in any form, and in the papers' own tables the retail concentration is ATM / ≤ 2 % OTM (Beckmeyer Table 1: > 2 % OTM is 1.4 % of retail call volume and 3.2 % of put volume; Amaya Table: "Below −2" moneyness 0.20 % of trades; de Silva: "primarily purchase at-the-money options"; Bryzgalova: "slightly OTM" 23 % of trades, sub-week 50 %) — the Compass band, not the lottery wing.

### 2.2 Evidence after the papers' samples, and what was not found

- The latest samples that touch the skewness characteristics are Aretz–Lin–Poon (*RoF* 2023; 1996-01 → 2019-06; implied skewness −0.20 [t −1.85] … −0.05 [−1.71] beside moneyness t ≥ 5) and Goyal–Saretto (*RFS* 2025; 1996-01 → 2022-12; skewness characteristics' IPCA alphas insignificant, every strategy's net alpha negative), both read by the sibling; Garcia-Ares–Muravyev (FMA 2025 [WP]; to 2023-08) via the L3 notes places the ex-rollover delta-hedged IVOL effect at −0.21 %/month (t −1.92) in the broad sample and +0.14 % in S&P 500 names. Félix–Kräussl–Stork (Tinbergen 16-022 [WP]; *J. Behavioral Finance* 2019 [PR]) is the only regime study: OTM single-stock call overpricing on 97 % of days 1998–2003-01, *under*pricing on 65 % of days 2003 → 2008-02, "less pervasive" to 2013-03 — "sample specific and not structural".
- **Not found by either lens** (the sibling's search ran out after locating the sources above; this note's search could not run at all): (i) a replication of the Boyer–Vorkink quintile spreads on data after 2013, on weekly (≤ 7-DTE) or 0DTE contracts, or at ask prices; (ii) any test of a MAX/IVOL/smirk exclusion applied *within* a fixed delta band and tenor, which is the only cell a Compass rule would occupy; (iii) any test on non-OptionMetrics data or at intraday quotes; (iv) Byun–Kim's and Bali–Murray's sample periods and cost treatment in primary form. These absences are stated as such, not as evidence of absence; the owner can send a follow-up to re-run the searches when the budget resets.

Reading for the data check: every construction is monthly or semi-monthly, uses OptionMetrics closing quotes, CRSP firm fields and vendor Greeks, and measures gross midpoint returns held to expiration on contracts that span the whole moneyness range. None measures the quantity Compass would use — the raw return of a 0.30–0.60-delta, ≤ 7-DTE contract over minutes to three sessions, conditional on a stock-level lottery rank — and there is no prior work at that tenor to calibrate an expected effect against.

## 4. Window length and power against the gates [LOCAL arithmetic]

- **What gate applies.** An exclusion rule has no expectancy, so GATE-v2's statistical gate (positive net expectancy, day-clustered lower bound > 0, out of sample, after multiplicity) and forward gate (≥ 150 sessions **and** ≥ 7 calendar months of paper trading with live NBBO logging) apply to the lane it filters, not to the rule; protocol §7's "≥ 150 out-of-sample trades over ≥ 3 months per reported cell" applies to the excluded-vs-retained cell. The H14 precedent is a matched-pair TRAIN/TEST measurement on a lane that existed. The long-option lanes are S01/S02/S09 (rejected, negative before costs), S03/S16b (zero), S12b (negative) and S11's pre-earnings cells (n ≤ 27). A filter applied to a lane whose expectation is set by an entry with no information (S04, S06) changes the mean by the excluded set's carry and cannot change the sign.
- **Power for the only applicable cell (S03, ≥ 5 DTE).** Per-contract P&L sd $62.4 (818 fills; ≈ $500 median premium, ≤ 3-session hold). A one-sided 95 % lower bound above zero on a two-group difference needs n per group = 2 × (1.645 σ / δ)²: **843 per group at $5 (1 % of premium), 5,268 at $2, 21,070 at $1.** The cell has 93 and 68. Filling every S03 session (the stratified pull covered SPY 468 / QQQ 294 / IWM 294 of 2,353 sessions; 14,884 signals fell on unpulled sessions) would raise the 7-DTE cell to roughly 161 × 2,353 / 352 ≈ 1,100 fills — enough for a $5 effect in-sample, not for the residual-skewness size the literature supports once moneyness is fixed (< 1 % of premium per week; the 2012-06 → 2017-05 extension adds five more years of the same product class).
- **Power for a single-name version.** S11's long cells at the full pull: ≈ 160 per structure, ≈ 32 per quintile, closing marks, 11 % half-spreads; the L3 data note's scaling (single-name option sd 80.6 %/month → ≈ 30 % over ≤ 3 sessions) gives ≈ 1,100 trades per quintile cell for a 1.5 %-of-premium effect and ≈ 10,000 at 0.5 %. There is no single-name long-option lane in the registry and no forward paper lane to validate a rule on.
- **Window for a gross replication (not the hypothesis).** Index weeklies: 2017-06 → 2026-10 cached on a stratified sample; extendable to 2012-06 (vendor) — the sibling's "run it as a data-quality diagnostic" option. Single names: the S11 cache's **33 holding months** (2024-01 → 2026-09) on event-selected root-dates; a Tier A monthly cross-section would give 172 formation dates / 170 holding months / 140 out-of-sample months under calendar-year folds from 2015 (the L2/L3 count), on a survivorship-biased universe until the 2013–2024 tiers exist, which wait on the 2012–2022 stock-EOD years that are not landed.
- **Multiplicity (A5 batch rule).** Three rules × thresholds × two return definitions × tenors; promotion of a follow-up cell needs the same sign every year and a Bonferroni-adjusted interval across the batch. The S03 by-year means already alternate sign (2017 −3.3 … 2025 +26.1 … 2026 −6.7); no exclusion cell could satisfy "same sign in every year" on 10–20 fills per year.
- **Data integrity (GATE-v2: full chains incl. extreme-move days, hashed).** The 0DTE cache qualifies (DATA-1 fixed); the S03 cache is a stratified session sample; the S11 cache is event-selected; none is a full per-name chain history.

## 5. Retail accessibility (brief; the sibling notes have the detail)

The exclusion is retail-accessible at zero execution cost: it removes candidates. The *premium* the three papers document is earned on the short side — writing the highest-skew, lowest-priced, zero-bid contracts (Boyer–Vorkink: "selling highly skewed options near the bid generally fails to capture the premium"; Bali–Murray: three-leg delta/vega-neutral assets on large caps, margin unaddressed) — which is naked single-name writing outside the Compass long-option universe (H15 scope note; A4 opened defined-risk structures only) and, at the measured quote state (item 1: zero bid), is not a marketable-order trade on either side. Institutional-only: the short side's margin and inventory capacity, midpoint execution at the papers' effective spreads (Muravyev–Pearson's timed execution), and trader-type data. Nothing in the data check changes the classification: the only retail-accessible content is "do not buy the wing", which the engine already does not.

1. **A gross moneyness-bucket diagnostic on the cached index chains, labelled data-quality** (the sibling's §4 suggestion, sized here): 10:00 NBBO mid *and* ask, hold to expiry (0DTE: settlement; weeklies: the expiring chain's intrinsic from the underlying close), by moneyness bucket and DTE, zero-bid contracts flagged and reported separately, 2017-06 → 2026-10 on the S03 sample and 2022-11 → 2026-10 on the 0DTE cache; one pass of engine time, no pull. 2. **The cheap sign check on the S11 controls** (1,789 random root-dates): smirk, MAX (prior 21 sessions, from the 2023 → 2026 lake) and CAPM-IVOL at the event close; 21-session raw return of the ATM call at the closing NBBO by quintile; three years, one regime, 11 % half-spreads, ≈ 54 root-dates per month. Worth an afternoon only to decide whether any single-name pull is worth its login-days; it cannot be gated (closing execution on single names is ruled out by `execution-costs.md` §2.2 / X4).
3. **Rule adoption without a registry lane** (the sibling's decision, unchanged by this lens): the Compass contract band (delta 0.30–0.60, ask ≥ $0.05, spread < 12 %) and H14 are the exclusion; strike "premium < 1 % of spot" from any future draft (it removes 80 % of index-weekly fills and 2 % of single-name 30–45-DTE candidates, in both cases by IV × √T); move the smirk to L5 and MAX/IVOL to L3's single IV − HV exclusion, to be adopted by default if a single-name long-option lane ever exists.
